Vocational Scenario or Context
You are working as a junior full stack developer at MediCore Solutions -an emerging healthcare software company that specialises in building digital systems for clinics, hospitals, and private medical practices.
MediCore Solutions has been commissioned by CareTrack Clinic -a mid-sized private medical centre with multiple specialist departments -to build a web-based Medical Record Management System (MRMS). The clinic currently relies on paper-based records and disconnected spreadsheets, which cause delays, data errors and difficulties in tracking patient histories across departments.
Before development begins, the technical director has asked you to first research and evaluate the tools and technologies used in full stack healthcare systems, then design and build the MRMS solution for CareTrack Clinic.
The MRMS must allow clinic staff to:
Create, view, update and delete Doctor profiles -name, specialty, department and contact details
Create, view, update and delete Patient records -personal details and their assigned doctor
Create, view, update and delete Disease/Diagnosis records -ICD code, description, severity and the linked patient
View a full Patient profile showing their assigned Doctor and all linked Disease/Diagnosis records
Search and filter across Doctors, Patients and Diseases
Access the system with role-based permissions: Administrators have full access; Clinicians can view and update Patient and Disease records only
The data model must reflect the following relationships:
A Doctor can have many Patients
A Patient is assigned to one Doctor
A Patient can have many Disease/Diagnosis records
A Disease/Diagnosis belongs to one Patient
Set Assignment Information
CareTrack Clinic provides a range of medical services across multiple specialist departments. The company wants a system that allows staff to:
Understand which doctors are available and manage their profiles
View and manage patient records and their assigned doctors
Record and track disease/diagnosis histories linked to patients
Submit and process new patient registrations
Access and update general clinical information
Generate and review patient diagnosis reports
Services offered:
General practice consultations
Specialist doctor appointments (cardiology, neurology, dermatology, orthopaedics)
Diagnostic services (blood tests, imaging, ECG)
Disease and diagnosis recording and management
Patient history tracking across departments
After-hours emergency contact service
Referral management between departments
Staff roles and access levels:
Administrator - full access to all Doctor, Patient and Disease records plus system settings
Clinician - view and update Patient and Disease/Diagnosis records only
Receptionist - register new Patients and view Doctor schedules
Set Assignment
You must complete ALL activities.
ACTIVITY 1 – suggested time 2 hours
The directors of CareTrack Clinic are eager to have a high-quality medical record management system and have asked you to provide them with information about how other similar systems meet the needs of their organisations and users.
You should research two existing healthcare or clinic management systems that have a similar purpose and/or operate in a similar area.
You must:
compare the design and features of the two identified systems
consider the suitability of the systems’ features and design for the intended purpose and audience (clinical staff, administrators, receptionists)
analyse how full stack development principles are used in each system
provide a supported evaluation of how well each system meets the needs of the users and the organisation
You should consider any positive or negative aspects of the two systems. Use specific examples to support the points you make in your report.
This activity covers learning aim A. A.P1, A.M1, A.D1
ACTIVITY 2 – suggested time 4 hours
The client would like to see some initial designs for the system before you start developing it.
Create comprehensive designs for the MRMS which include:
identification of user requirements
visual designs for the key screens (Doctor list, Patient profile, Disease/Diagnosis management, Dashboard)
alternative design options
technical documentation including an ERD and data dictionary
You should use the Set Assignment Information and what you learned through your research in Activity 1 to inform your designs.
This activity covers learning aim B. B.P2
ACTIVITY 3 – suggested time 2 hours
You now need to seek feedback in order to improve the quality and appropriateness of your designs. 
You must:
show your designs to others
gather and analyse the feedback you receive
identify areas of the designs that could be improved
further develop/improve your designs using the feedback given
You must provide justification of your design decisions, including how you responded to any feedback you received. 
You should include:
how the designs will meet user needs and be fit for purpose
consideration of alternative design options
which suggested changes you made, which you didn’t and why
This could be in the form of annotations on the designs or as an additional piece of written evidence.
This activity covers learning aim B. B.P3, B.M2
ACTIVITY 4 – suggested time 10 hours
You must now develop the MRMS for the client.
During the development of the system you must:
create the full stack application using suitable tools and technologies
implement Doctor CRUD, Patient CRUD and Disease/Diagnosis CRUD with correct relational data linking all three entities
implement role-based access control for Administrators and Clinicians
use appropriate test plans, test schedules and data to test the system for functionality, compatibility (with different devices and browsers) and usability (including user testing)
You must also seek feedback in order to improve the quality and appropriateness of your system. 
You must:
show your system to others
gather and analyse the feedback you receive
identify areas of the system that could be improved
further develop/improve your system using the feedback given
During testing and feedback activities you should collect any results and feedback you have received and document how you have responded to any problems or errors identified. 
This evidence should include:
‘before’ and ‘after’ versions of the system showing notable changes made during development
details of how the system has been ‘optimised’ based on results of testing and user feedback
This activity covers learning aim C. C.P4, C.P5, C.M3
ACTIVITY 5 – suggested time 2 hours
The development of the system is now complete and you need to review the extent to which the project meets the client’s needs.
Write an evaluation of the design, development and optimisation of the MRMS. 
Your evaluation must:
consider how effectively the system: 
meets CareTrack Clinic’s requirements
appeals to and meets the needs of the target audience (clinical staff, administrators, receptionists)
achieves its stated purpose
compare features of the final system with other solutions that could have been used
provide a detailed and balanced evaluation of what the system does well and areas for improvement
review your own skills, performance and behaviours and the impact that this had on the development of the final system
Your evaluation should be supported by evidence from all stages of the project to reach conclusions and suggest developments.
This activity covers learning aims B and C. C.P6, BC.D2, BC.D3
Checklist of evidence required
Learners must submit the following evidence to support assessment of this assignment:
Completed final report - A structured report covering all required activities, written in clear and professional language, demonstrating understanding of full stack development principles applied to the MRMS.
Written report of analysis results - A detailed written analysis presenting findings from research into existing healthcare management systems, design decisions, testing outcomes, and evaluation activities.
Evidence of data preparation and refinement - Screenshots showing the preparation, organisation and refinement of information used during the project (e.g. database schema setup, seed data, test plans, CRUD operation testing, optimisation steps).
Client-focused report - A clear and concise report prepared for CareTrack Clinic management, explaining the MRMS solution, its key features - including Doctor, Patient and Disease/Diagnosis management - and how all client requirements have been met.
Learning Aim A: Explore tools and technologies for full stack development
A.P1 - Compare the full stack tools and technologies used in two different healthcare management systems, considering their suitability for the intended users and organisational purpose.
A.M1 - Analyse how full stack development tools and technologies are used to create efficient, high-performance medical record management systems that meet client requirements.
A.D1 - Evaluate how full stack development tools and technologies are applied in the design and deployment of high-performance medical record management systems that meet client requirements.
Learning Aim B: Design a fullstack solution to meet client
requirements
B.P2 - Produce full stack designs for the MRMS that meet CareTrack Clinic’s client requirements, including ERD, data dictionary, wireframes and technical documentation.
B.P3 - Review the MRMS design proposals with others to identify and inform improvements.
B.M2 - Justify design decisions for the MRMS, explaining how they meet user needs and are fit for purpose in a clinical environment.
BC.D2 - Evaluate design decisions and the optimised MRMS against CareTrack Clinic’s client requirements, explaining how they meet user needs and are fit for purpose.
Learning Aim C - Develop a fullstack solution to meet client requirements
C.P4 - Produce a full stack MRMS for an intended audience and test it for functionality, compatibility, usability and purpose.
C.P5 - Test the MRMS for functionality and usability, including Doctor, Patient and Disease/Diagnosis CRUD operations and role-based access control.
C.M3 - Optimise the MRMS to meet CareTrack Clinic’s client requirements based on testing results and user feedback.
C.P6 - Review the extent to which the MRMS meets CareTrack Clinic’s client requirements.
BC.D3 - Demonstrate individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management in the design, development and review of the MRMS.
